20

Flying about the cosmos,
'Twas not the sun that did greet me.
I had arrived in Nirvana,
Where heavenly maidens do speak.
I sat upon the edge of Sirius' atmosphere,
To burn mine ego.
Now Nirvana hath vanished,
For mine ego was burned.
Who would have thought I could not endure?
To pass through the nebula is no difficult thing,
'Tis mine ego that doth blaze.
I burn it.
It doth not vanish,
But—
It burneth ever more fiercely.
— Rahma
